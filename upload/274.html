MAT 274

Exam work upload

Finish and submit your exam on the computer first. Then come back here. You have 15 minutes after you submit to send me your work.

Open the exam in OHM

This goes through Brightspace and will ask you to log in. You do not need a separate OHM password.

How to send me your work

  1. Scan your pages, do not photograph them. On an iPhone, open the Notes app, start a new note, tap the camera icon and choose Scan Documents. On Android, open Google Drive, tap the plus sign and choose Scan. Either one straightens the page, fixes the lighting, and puts every page into one clean PDF. A plain camera photo is harder to read and often will not open on my end.
  2. Tap the button above and log into Brightspace. It will take you to the exam in OHM.
  3. Open the work upload. Since your attempt is already submitted, OHM will offer you a place to add your work. Choose the file you just scanned.
  4. Hit Save, and wait for it to finish. You should see your file listed by name. If you do not see it listed, it did not go through.

A few things worth knowing

Yes, your phone is allowed for this. Picking it up to scan and upload your work is an expected part of the exam. The recording will show you using it, and that is fine.

Do not open the exam on your phone before you have submitted it on the computer. Being in the same attempt on two devices at once can confuse OHM and put your answers at risk. Submit first, then scan.

If the button opens the Brightspace app instead of your browser, come back to this page, press and hold the button, and choose Open in Browser. The Pulse app cannot launch OHM.

If anything goes wrong, email me the scan directly so it is timestamped, and tell me what happened. Do not spend your exam time troubleshooting this.
